# Meeting to Actions

Extract only what the meeting record supports.

1. Summarize the purpose and outcomes in a few sentences.
2. Separate **decisions**, **action items**, **open questions**, and **discussion points**.
3. For each action, capture owner, deliverable, due date, and dependency only when stated. Mark missing fields as unassigned or unspecified rather than inventing them.
4. Preserve dissent, conditions, and decision rationale when they affect execution.
5. Flag conflicting statements and unclear references for confirmation.
6. Produce a concise follow-up note suitable for sharing with attendees.

Do not infer commitments from tentative language. Do not send messages, create tasks, or update calendars unless explicitly asked and the relevant tool is available. Keep confidential meeting details within the intended audience.
