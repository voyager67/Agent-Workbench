# Decision Brief

Frame a decision so the accountable person can choose with clear trade-offs.

Include:

1. **Decision needed:** the exact choice and deadline/trigger if known.
2. **Context:** objective, constraints, and why a decision is needed now.
3. **Options:** a small set of viable alternatives, including do-nothing or defer when relevant.
4. **Evaluation:** compare options against agreed criteria such as cost, risk, reversibility, complexity, performance, and time.
5. **Recommendation:** choose one option and explain why; state confidence and assumptions.
6. **Consequences:** what the choice enables, what it rules out, rollback path, and follow-up actions.

Avoid false precision and arbitrary weighted scores. Cite evidence for factual claims and label estimates. If values or constraints are unknown, surface them rather than choosing silently. A recommendation is not authorization to execute the decision.
